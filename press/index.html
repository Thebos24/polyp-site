About Polyp

Polyp: Coral Growth Journal is an iPhone app for reef aquarium keepers. Every coral, fish or full-tank view gets its own photo timeline, with the days between photos and its total age. A ghost overlay in the camera shows the last photo on top of the live view, so every shot lines up and growth is easy to see. Old photos can be imported with their original dates, and timelines can be shared as collages. Polyp has no accounts, no tracking and no subscription: it is free for 1 tank and 8 corals, with one-time packs for more.

About the maker

Polyp is made by Sander Bos, a reef keeper in the Netherlands, who wanted to see his frags grow and was tired of losing coral photos in his camera roll.
