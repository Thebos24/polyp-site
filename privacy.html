Polyp privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Polyp does not collect any data about you. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network calls of its own.

Your photos and information

The photos you take or import, and the names, dates and tags you enter, are stored on your iPhone. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party.

iCloud Drive backup

If iCloud Drive is on, Polyp keeps a copy of your photos in the Polyp folder of your own iCloud Drive. This is stored by Apple in your Apple Account under Apple's terms. The developer cannot see it.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take coral photos inside Polyp.
  • Photo library: to import photos you choose, and, only if you turn the setting on, to save a copy of new photos to Photos or to delete originals after import. Polyp sees only the photos you pick.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Polyp receives only whether a pack is unlocked, not your payment details.

Children

Polyp collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions: imandidesign@gmail.com.
