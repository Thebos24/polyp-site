Common questions

Where are my photos? Inside Polyp, not in your camera roll. Polyp also keeps a copy in your iCloud Drive, in the folder Polyp, in the Files app.

I got a new phone or reinstalled the app. Open Polyp while signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on. Polyp offers to restore from the backup on the first screen, or in Settings.

I bought a pack on another device. Open Settings in Polyp and choose Restore purchase.

Can I use Polyp on Android or iPad? Not yet. Polyp is for iPhone.
