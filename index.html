Your coral photos, finally in order

You already take coral photos. Then they vanish between screenshots, receipts and holiday pictures. Polyp keeps each coral's photos together and shows how it changes.

Every coral gets its own timeline

Add a coral, fish or the full tank, and its photos line up in date order.

  • Days between photos and the total age of each coral
  • Play growth: flip through the photos like a flipbook
  • Tags like Fragged, Moved, Bleaching or Recovering

Line up every shot

The camera shows your last photo as a see-through ghost or an outline over the live view. Match it, tap, done. When the framing matches, the growth jumps out.

Bring your old photos, with their real dates

Import the coral photos already on your phone. Polyp reads the date each one was taken, so a coral's history starts on the day of its first picture, even if that was years ago. Afterwards your camera roll can stay free of coral photos.

Share the growth

Make a collage of 4, 6 or 9 photos with dates and day counts, and send it to your reef friends or your group chat.

Coming in version 1.1

Blue-light photos that look normal

Reef lights turn every photo blue. Adjust fixes it with warmth, tint and an Auto button, and "Same as last photo" keeps a whole timeline consistent. Your original photo is never changed.

Safe in your own iCloud Drive

Polyp copies every original photo to the Polyp folder in your iCloud Drive, in folders named after your tanks and corals. New phone or reinstalled the app? Restore everything in one tap.
