Common questions

Where are my photos?
Inside Polyp, not in your camera roll. Polyp also keeps a copy in your iCloud Drive, in the folder Polyp, which you can open in the Files app.
I got a new phone or reinstalled the app.
Open Polyp while signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on. Polyp offers to restore from the backup on the first screen, or later in Settings.
I bought a pack on another device.
Open Settings in Polyp and choose Restore purchase.
How many corals can I add for free?
The free version covers 1 tank and 8 corals or fish, with unlimited photos. Polyp Plus and Polyp Unlimited are one-time purchases. There is no subscription.
Can I use Polyp on Android or iPad?
Not yet. Polyp is for iPhone.
